The prescription

One family, two voices.

The tool becomes a neutral, fast, 2026 tech product — Instrument Sans, near-black on near-white, one electric coral accent, 8px geometry, real icons, dialogs and toasts. The artifact — the couple's website, invitations, RSVP cards — keeps the romance: Instrument Serif, ivory, gold. The serif appears in the app only where the couple's content shows through: template previews, site names, invitation cards. That contrast is the brand.
